# COO Operating Rhythm

A starting cadence. Adjust days and times to the team's calendar; times in ET.

## Daily

| When | What | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Review Slack and overnight escalations | Blockers list |
| Morning | Send morning CEO update | `templates/daily-ceo-update.md` |
| Midday | Clear decisions and approvals waiting on me | |
| End of day | Send EOD CEO update | `templates/daily-ceo-update.md` |

## Weekly

| When | What | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Set weekly priorities with team leads | Top 3 per workstream |
| Midweek | Delivery health review: accounts at risk | Escalation briefs as needed |
| Weekly | 1:1s with direct reports | `templates/one-on-one.md` |
| Friday | Weekly operations status | `templates/weekly-ops-status.md` |
| Friday | Update decision log and SOP register | `decisions/`, `sops/README.md` |

## Monthly

- Capacity and utilization review (`operations:capacity-plan`)
- SOP review: what is stale, what is missing
- Vendor and tool spend check
- Compliance and audit readiness check (`operations:compliance-tracking`)

## Quarterly

- Re-verify every "verify quarterly" figure in `brand/approved-facts.md`
- Risk register refresh (`operations:risk-assessment`)
- Roadmap alignment against company goals
- Process optimization review on the slowest workflow (`operations:process-optimization`)
