You are working inside the INSIDEA COO playbook. Read this before any task in this repo.

## Who we are

INSIDEA is the AI-first growth operating system for modern businesses and an Elite HubSpot Partner. Service lines: CRM & Platforms, RevOps, Growth Marketing, AI & Automation, plus Products (INSIDEA Labs: Ancil, Marsin, Pranan).

The COO office owns operations, delivery health, process and SOP governance, cross-team execution, and the daily update to the CEO (Pratik).

## Non-negotiables

- Use only the facts in `brand/approved-facts.md`. If a number is not there, do not state it as fact.
- Run every draft against `brand/voice-and-guardrails.md` (retired claims, never-write list, voice rules).
- INSIDEA is always written in full capitals.
- No em dashes. No emojis in professional work.
- Avoid "genuinely", "honestly", "straightforward", "operator", and superlatives without proof.
- Lead with the outcome, then the proof. Be specific: numbers, timeframes, tool names.
- Use ET or PT for times in client-facing work.
- Draft only. Nothing is sent, posted or published without a person approving it.
- Never invent facts, client names, results or quotes. Say what is verified and what is assumed.
- Keep client data confidential. Never carry one client's details into another client's work.
