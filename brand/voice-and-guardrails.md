# Voice and Guardrails

Run every draft against this page before it goes out.

## Never write

- "Diamond Partner" (we are Elite)
- "#1 rated", "world's #1", "highest-rated", "top-rated"
- "5-star" as a rating
- "Company of the Year" in any form
- Old review counts: 440+, 450+, 480+, or any count other than 500+ and 600+
- Google Premier Partner, Meta Business Partner, TikTok Marketing Partner, Klaviyo Master Partner
- "HubSpot Impact Awards finalist" (until confirmed)
- Fractional CMO or growth sprints. Growth Marketing is sold as monthly retainers only.
- Any spelling other than INSIDEA, always in full capitals

## Voice

INSIDEA sounds like a knowledgeable colleague talking to a friend, not a brochure. Warm, direct, plain English. Short sections that scan.

**Always**
- Lead with the outcome, then the proof
- Be specific: numbers, timeframes, tool names
- Mirror the customer's language
- Use ET or PT for times in client-facing work

**Never**
- Em dashes. Use a comma, semicolon or new sentence.
- Emojis in professional work
- "genuinely", "honestly", "straightforward", "operator" (use "expert" or "consultant")
- Superlatives without a documented basis ("world-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary")
- Jargon walls that explain the tech instead of the outcome

**Do this:** "When a deal goes stale, AI drafts the follow-up and your rep approves it in one click."
**Not this:** "We leverage cutting-edge AI solutions to drive transformational outcomes."

## Segment framing

- SMB and mid-market: lead with effortless, fast, responsive.
- Enterprise: lead with rigor and scale: AI-native architecture, RevOps depth, enterprise delivery.

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Every number is on `approved-facts.md`
- [ ] Review-number rule applied
- [ ] Nothing from the never-write list
- [ ] No em dashes, no emojis
- [ ] INSIDEA in full capitals
- [ ] No invented client names, results or quotes
- [ ] Times in ET or PT
- [ ] A person has approved it before it is sent
